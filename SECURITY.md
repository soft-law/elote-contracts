# Security policy

`elote_pool` holds depositor funds. If you find a vulnerability, please report it privately:

- GitHub: https://github.com/soft-law/elote-contracts/security/advisories/new
- Do not open a public issue for a security problem.

We acknowledge reports within 72 hours and keep you informed while we fix and deploy. The program embeds this policy on chain through `security.txt` (`solana-security-txt`).

## Scope

- `programs/elote_pool` (the on-chain program, program id `BGdJQxxJoHiLH2VS6HAGHe5kQuKKq9dwrPeX7DEEwYyM`)
- `clients/js` (`@softlaw/elote-pool-client`)

## Deployments

See "Deployments and upgrade authority" in the README for the deployed clusters, the upgrade authority and how to verify the on-chain build against this source. The program is deployed on devnet only and holds test tokens.

The program was audited on 2026-11-01 and re-audited on 2026-11-02; the reports are in `docs/audits/` on the audit branches, and `tests/audit.rs` and `tests/reaudit.rs` hold the regression tests of their findings.

## Roles and permissions

| Role | How it is identified | What it can do |
|---|---|---|
| Pool admin | `Pool.admin`, a signer fixed at `init_pool` and part of the pool's address; it cannot be changed | `open_line` (approve a credit line of up to `max_line_bps` of the NAV to any borrower other than itself, any number of times), `write_off` (recognise a loss once the line is overdue past the grace), `suspend_draws` (close a line's draw window now), `cancel_line` (clear an approved line nobody drew), `set_paused`, `set_attestor` (replace the attestor; never with itself), `set_collection_period` (never below 30 days), `set_cancel_delay` (never below 7 days; a pending request keeps its deadline), `request_cancellation` (record the backstop's request on a pagaré carrying the PAGADO mark, then cancel it alone after the delay), `cancel_pagare` (as the holder of the pagarés in the pool: extinguish a pagaré carrying the PAGADO mark against a receipt, with the attestor; the procurador of record may do the same through its program address), `endorse_pagare` (endoso en procuración, with the attestor; never to the zero key, the pool, the admin or the attestor), `revoke_procuracion`, `court_annotation` (with the attestor), `annotate_payment` (which also replaces the payer's provisional receipt hash on a pagaré marked paid and not yet burnt) |
| Attestor | `Pool.attestor`, named at `init_pool`, rotated by the admin with `set_attestor`, which the new key co-signs; the agreements engine's key, never the zero key, the admin, a borrower or a procurador | Co-signs `open_line` (attesting the signed agreement matches the arguments), `draw` (attesting the pagaré behind the hash was signed and is conserved; its client derives the pagaré PDAs, so the attestor's client is the canonicality guard), `cancel_pagare` (attesting the receipt behind the hash and that the pagaré's registry was archived off chain), `endorse_pagare` (the signed endoso and the endorsee's verification) and `court_annotation` (the court document); may sign `annotate_payment` alone (attaching a receipt to a payment entry, which the admin may do too). It can block every opening, draw, endoso and court annotation by withholding its signature, and delay a cancellation until the backstop's deadline; it cannot move funds or títulos |
| Borrower | `CreditLine.borrower`, a co-signer of `open_line` | `draw` tranches of its approved line, with the attestor, while the draw window is open (until the first due date, or the term on a bullet line) |
| Sponsor | `CreditLine.sponsor`, an optional co-signer of `open_line` | Nothing after the opening; it is a recorded party, not an authority |
| Depositor | Any signer holding the pool asset | `deposit` and `redeem` at the current net asset value, subject to the pause and to the vault's liquidity, and, on a permissioned series, to holding a thawed share account |
| Share-mint freeze authority | The freeze authority of the admin-supplied share mint (Token ACL's `MintConfig` in production, driven by the allow list the backend feeds; the program does not check who it is) | Thaw a holder's share account (admit an investor) or freeze it (bar deposits and redemptions); thaw the dead-shares account before the first deposit. It cannot move funds or shares |
| Payer (relayer) | Any signer passed as `payer` to `draw`, `endorse_pagare`, `revoke_procuracion` or `cancel_pagare` | Pays the rent of the pagaré asset and its record at the draw and any rent a Core plugin change needs, and receives the asset's rent back when the pagaré is cancelled; nothing else |
| Procurador | `Pagare.procurador`, written by `endorse_pagare` and cleared by `revoke_procuracion` and the cancellation | `cancel_pagare` on that pagaré, with the attestor, signing as itself while the program signs for the burn delegate's address `["procurador", pagare, procurador]`; never the backstop path |
| Anyone | No check | `repay` any drawn line, `mark_in_collection` once a scheduled line has missed `max_missed` installments plus the grace |
| Upgrade authority | The BPF loader's upgrade authority for the program id | Replace the program. Today a single key; see the README |

No instruction closes a `Pool`, a `CreditLine` or a `Pagare` account (a cancelled pagaré's record stays as the "PAGADO" mark; only the Core asset is burnt, by the Core program), and no instruction moves tokens out of the vault other than `draw` (to the borrower named on an admin-approved line) and `redeem` (to the holder burning shares).

## Trust assumptions

- **The pool admin is trusted with the whole vault.** Depositors lend to whoever the admin approves. Each line is capped at `max_line_bps` of the NAV at its opening (`LineTooLarge`; 1,000 bps, a tenth, in production pools), and `open_line` refuses the admin key as the borrower (`BorrowerIsAdmin`), so the one-key path "open one line to self, draw the vault" is closed. There is still no exposure limit per borrower across lines and no allowlist of borrowers: a compromised or dishonest admin can open several lines to wallets it controls and drain the vault a tenth at a time. The cap is measured against the NAV at the moment of `open_line`, which a deposit in the same transaction, redeemed right after, inflates; so the cap bounds honest mistakes and the size of any single line the app proposes, and is not a defence against the admin.
- **The upgrade authority is trusted with the program.** Until the authority is a multisig and builds are verified, the deployed program can be replaced by one key.
- **The pool asset is trusted, within a policy.** `init_pool` accepts a classic SPL Token or a Token-2022 mint, under the token program the caller names, and refuses a mint whose extensions take or hide value or would freeze the vault at creation: transfer fee, permanent delegate, transfer hook, non-transferable, any confidential extension, mint close authority, and a frozen default account state (`AssetMintNotAllowed`). Two issuer controls that can stop the vault without touching the program are accepted and recorded in `Pool.asset_controls` (decision 15): a freeze authority (MEXe has one) and the `Pausable` extension. The asset's mint authority can still inflate the supply; nothing on chain checks the issuer.
- **The share mint is the admin's, within a policy.** `init_pool` validates the mint the admin supplies (pool PDA as mint authority, empty supply, the asset's decimals, no fee, hook, close authority, permanent delegate, interest-bearing rate or confidential extension: `ShareMintNotAllowed`). With `DefaultAccountState = Frozen` the shares are permissioned (`Pool.permissioned` and `PoolInitialized.permissioned`, the fact anyone can check): every share account is born frozen and the mint's freeze authority decides who is thawed. The program records nothing about that authority; the first mainnet pool must be created with a Token-2022 frozen-by-default mint whose freeze authority is Token ACL's `MintConfig` (decision 15, see `docs/runbook.md`).
- **Depositors accept first-come-first-served liquidity.** Redemptions are paid from the vault balance only; principal lent out is part of the NAV but is not liquid until it is repaid.
- **The agreement hash is an opaque commitment.** The program does not verify what the hash stands for; the agreements service that produced it does.
- **Metaplex Core is a dependency.** The pagaré assets live in a Core collection the admin creates with the pool PDA as update authority (`init_pool` checks owner, kind and authority and admits only a bare collection, no plugin header and nothing minted, since Core applies a collection's permanent plugins to every asset in it: `CollectionNotAllowed`). Every Core call goes through the Core program (`CoREENxT6tW1HoK8ypY1SxRMZTcVPm7R94rH4PZNhX7d`), signed by the pool PDA or by a procurador's program address: `CreateV2` at `draw` (one per tranche: the asset owned by the pool PDA, a frozen permanent freeze delegate whose authority is the collection's, immutable metadata, locked attributes and an `AddBlocker`; no transfer or burn delegate); `AddPluginV1` at the first `endorse_pagare`, which adds an owner-managed `BurnDelegate` with authority `["procurador", pagare, procurador]`, and `ApprovePluginAuthorityV1` at a later one; `RevokePluginAuthorityV1` at `revoke_procuracion`, which returns that authority to the owner; and at `cancel_pagare` `UpdatePluginV1`, which thaws the asset, then `BurnV1`. There is no permanent burn or transfer delegate and no AppData plugin; the program never transfers the asset. Its upgrade authority, Metaplex, can change how those assets behave; the tests run the pinned release `release/core@0.15.3`, and `tests/core_capabilities.rs` pins what that release lets each party do after creation (hand off or extinguish a permanent delegate, thaw, move the update authority, owner-managed delegates, a burn delegate held by a program address), so a Core change that alters the pagaré's powers fails the suite. The attestor's co-signature on `cancel_pagare` has a backstop so the debtor is never left without receipt or title if the attestor fails: a cancellation request recorded by the holder and, after the pool's `cancel_delay_seconds`, a cancellation by the holder alone with the requested receipt and the same checks. The holder is the pool, so the backstop is the admin cancelling alone after the delay; a procurador never cancels without the attestor. In `cancel_pagare` the program picks the branch by the signer: the admin as the owner, or the procurador the sidecar records (`Pagare.procurador`, written by the endoso and cleared by the revocation and the extinction); on the procurador's branch it derives the program address `["procurador", pagare, procurador]`, compares it with the account given and signs for it in `BurnV1`. Nothing in the asset's plugin registry is read on chain (it sits behind the variable-length name and URI); Core's own refusal of any authority but the owner or the approved delegate is the second check, atomic with the PAGADO mark.

## Centralization risks

| Risk | Effect | Planned mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Single admin key | Loss or compromise of the key: the vault can be drained (open a line to a second wallet, draw), or the pool can be paused forever | Multisig as admin; operator and pauser roles with caps; two-step origination so the borrower signs separately |
| Admin is immutable | The admin is part of the pool PDA seeds, so it cannot be rotated; a compromised pool must be abandoned and a new one created | Roles account with two-step handover |
| Pause with no exit | While paused, depositors cannot redeem and borrowers cannot draw an approved line while its term keeps running, so a long pause can let an approved line lapse at `term_end`; repayments still arrive. A lost admin key while paused locks every depositor | Pauser role that cannot unpause, multisig unpause, a written procedure |
| Single upgrade key | The program can be replaced by one key | Squads multisig as upgrade and IDL authority; verified builds |

## Known risks by design

These are documented limits of the current program, not open vulnerabilities. All but the hold, clock and `accelerated_at` items are pinned by a test in `programs/elote_pool/tests/known_limits.rs`; the planned changes are on the docs site under "Roadmap and limits".

- **Step-function NAV.** Interest counts when it is collected and a loss when it is written off, so the share price moves in steps. A deposit placed just before a known repayment captures part of that interest; a redemption between `mark_in_collection` and `write_off` exits at the pre-loss value and leaves the loss to the remaining holders.
- **No hold on a drawn line.** The admin can clear an approved line nobody drew (`cancel_line`) and close any line's draw window early (`suspend_draws`). A line with a tranche drawn cannot be revoked or frozen: it is repaid, collected or written off, and `repay` is never blocked.
- **No recovery after write-off.** A written-off line takes no repayment. If every line is written off and the NAV reaches zero while shares exist, `deposit` fails until value is transferred into the vault directly.
- **Collection can be unreachable.** `mark_in_collection` requires `covered + max_missed <= installments`; on a short schedule a borrower who paid early installments and then stopped can only be written off after the term.
- **First-deposit floor on low-decimal assets.** The first-deposit minimum is 1.0 of the asset, but never fewer base units than 1,000 times the dead shares (1,000,000), so on an asset with fewer than six decimals the minimum is more than one whole unit (10,000.00 on a two-decimal asset). Pinned by `first_deposit_minimum_is_one_unit_of_the_asset_with_a_dead_shares_floor`.
- **Late interest timing.** Late interest is computed at the second of the repayment, so a client must pad a repayment that is meant to settle it, or the program rejects it with `LateInterestUnpaid`.
- **Exact-second gates on a leader-set clock.** Every time gate (the draw window, the second an installment counts as due, grace ends, the collection period) compares `Clock::unix_timestamp` at an exact boundary, and since Alpenglow the leader sets that timestamp within a bounded range, so a gate can move by a few seconds at a leader's discretion. The exposure is one second of interest or a window edge; clients pad their quotes.
- **One field, two meanings.** `CreditLine.accelerated_at` is both the acceleration of the balance (decision 28) and the start of the collection period, written by `mark_in_collection` at the same instant. Acceleration and the start of collection cannot be set apart.
- **Non-canonical pagaré bumps.** `draw` takes the two pagaré PDA bumps from the client and verifies each with one `create_program_address`, so its compute is deterministic; a valid non-canonical bump is accepted too and puts the asset where `find_program_address` never looks. The attestor derives the canonical bump and co-signs every draw, and the `PagareIssued` event and the sidecar carry the asset's address, so nothing downstream has to derive it (`limit_draw_accepts_a_valid_non_canonical_pagare_bump`).
- **A concurrent payment makes an in-flight repayment retry.** `repay` takes exactly the pagaré records the payment touches, which the client computes from the line when it builds the transaction. A payment by anyone that lands first (a stranger's single unit is enough) can change that set, and the in-flight `repay` fails with `PagareAccountsMismatch`; nothing moves, and the client re-reads `pagares_paid` and the line and retries. It matters only at a deadline, such as the grace end before `mark_in_collection` (`limit_a_payment_landing_first_makes_an_in_flight_repay_retry`).
- **Five pagaré records per repayment.** Nothing in the program bounds how many pagaré records one `repay` touches (up to `MAX_DRAWS`), and each costs compute: six leave less than 5,000 CU under the project's 100,000 CU ceiling and eight run over it. The client splits a payment so that each `repay` touches at most five records (`MAX_SIDECARS_PER_REPAY` in `clients/js/src/pagare.ts`); the amount is free, so a split always works (`limit_a_repay_touching_more_than_five_pagare_records_must_be_split`; `tests/compute.rs` pins five under the ceiling with a 5,000 CU margin).

## What the program does enforce

- Every account is typed and owner-checked by Anchor; the pool, the vault, the share mint, the dead-share account and every line are PDAs validated by seeds and stored bumps; token accounts are checked against the pool's mints and the signer; the CPI targets are the token programs (classic SPL Token or Token-2022, through Anchor's `TokenInterface`, with `transfer_checked` carrying the mint's decimals), the system program (the sweep of stray lamports and the sidecar's `create_account` in `draw`) and Metaplex Core (`CreateV2` in `draw`; `AddPluginV1` or `ApprovePluginAuthorityV1` in `endorse_pagare`; `RevokePluginAuthorityV1` in `revoke_procuracion`; `UpdatePluginV1`, the thaw, and `BurnV1` in `cancel_pagare`; all signed by the pool PDA except the procurador's `BurnV1`, signed for its program address); after each token CPI the balances read for events are reloaded.
- Every money instruction (`deposit`, `redeem`, `draw`, `repay`, `write_off`) ends with `Pool::check()`: the NAV (`vault + outstanding_principal`) fits a `u64`, and where the share mint is an account (`deposit`, `redeem`, `repay`, `write_off`) its supply is zero or at least the dead shares. A failure is `InvariantBroken` and the transaction is refused. This is defence in depth: no instruction today can break either invariant, and the check is there so that a future change which does is refused on chain rather than found in an audit.
- All arithmetic is checked; products go through `u128`; both share-accounting legs round down in the pool's favour; late interest is capped at the overdue amount per settlement and at the principal over the line's life.
- The first deposit locks dead shares and must meet a minimum, so a dust deposit cannot set the opening share price and a donation cannot round later deposits to zero.
- Events are emitted through `emit_cpi!` (self-CPI), not logs, with `nav_after` on every deposit, redemption, repayment and write-off, and `NavUpdated` last in each.
